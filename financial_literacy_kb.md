# Comprehensive Digital Financial Literacy & Fraud Prevention Knowledge Base

## 1. UPI Architecture & Security Protocols (NPCI Framework)
- UPI PIN Fundamentals: A UPI PIN is strictly an authentication key for authorizing outgoing funds (debits). No receiving transaction (credit), cashback claim, or refund ever requires entering a UPI PIN.
- QR Code Architecture: QR codes encode payment destination addresses (VPAs). Scanning any QR code prompts a debit payment request, never an incoming credit.
- Collect Request Exploits: Scammers send "Collect Requests" with names formatted like "Customer Care Refund ₹5000". Approving the notification transfers ₹5000 to the scammer.
- Standard Limits: Daily peer-to-peer limit is ₹1,00,000. Capital market, insurance, and IPO transactions permit limits up to ₹5,00,000.

## 2. Advanced Threat Analysis & Financial Cybercrime
- Digital Arrest Exploits: Fraudulent actors impersonating CBI, Cyber Police, or Customs via Skype/WhatsApp video calls with fake uniform backdrops. They claim international parcels contain narcotics or bank accounts are flagged for money laundering, demanding money to a "safe escrow account". Genuine enforcement agencies never conduct judicial proceedings via video call.
- Malicious Sideloaded APKs: Phishing SMS alleging urgent SIM card deactivation or electricity bill cancellation. The links prompt installing APKs (e.g., `SBI_Rewards.apk`, `QuickSupport.apk`) that intercept two-factor SMS OTPs.
- Screen-Sharing Scams: Scammers posing as bank helpline agents instruct victims to download AnyDesk or TeamViewer to "resolve failed UPI transactions", capturing login credentials in real time.
- Emergency Incident Escalation: Dial National Cybercrime Helpline **1930** immediately or report at **cybercrime.gov.in** within the first 2 hours ("Golden Period") to freeze fraudulent account transfers.

## 3. Personal Financial Planning, Emergency Reserves & 50/30/20 Rule
- Needs Allocation (50%): Housing rent/mortgage, baseline groceries, utilities, existing loan EMIs, primary medical insurance.
- Wants Allocation (30%): Discretionary spending, dining out, lifestyle subscriptions, travel, luxury purchases.
- Wealth Building & Savings (20%): Long-term debt payoff, emergency fund maintenance, Systematic Investment Plans (SIPs) in mutual funds, Public Provident Fund (PPF).
- Emergency Reserve Protocol: Allocate 3 to 6 months of mandatory living expenses in high-liquidity, low-risk instruments (high-interest savings accounts or liquid mutual funds).

## 4. Debt Management, Interest Rates & Credit Scoring
- Simple vs. Compound Interest Dynamics: Simple interest accrues strictly on principal ($A = P(1 + rt)$). Compound interest charges interest on accumulated past interest ($A = P(1 + r/n)^{nt}$), exponentially increasing the total repayment amount over prolonged loan tenures.
- Regulated vs Predatory Lending: Legitimate commercial bank personal loans range between 10.5% and 18% APR. Predatory loan apps charging daily interest rates or APR exceeding 36% while demanding contact list permissions operate outside RBI oversight.
- Credit Score (CIBIL): Scored from 300 to 900. A score of 750+ secures prime borrowing rates, lower processing fees, and expedited approvals.
